# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in PocketPrint welcoming and
respectful for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, identity,
or personal characteristics.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Assume good intent while being open to feedback.
- Focus criticism on ideas, code, and behaviour rather than people.
- Accept responsibility, apologise when mistakes happen, and learn from them.
- Respect privacy; do not publish another person's personal information.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and sustained disruptive
  behaviour.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Threats, unwanted sexual attention, or other conduct that would be
  inappropriate in a professional community.

## Scope

This code applies in project repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions,
and other spaces where someone represents the PocketPrint project.

## Reporting

Report behaviour that violates this code to the project maintainer through a
private GitHub communication channel. For security vulnerabilities, follow
[SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md) instead.

The maintainer will review reports as fairly and promptly as possible and may
remove comments, close discussions, reject contributions, or restrict
participation when necessary. Reports will be handled with discretion, but
absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.
